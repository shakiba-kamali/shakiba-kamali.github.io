DC-DC Power Board Design in Altium

Independent electrical design

What
Designed a 40 to 160 V DC input board to provide a regulated 15 V output through a DC-DC stage and linear regulator.
How
Selected filtering, fuses, capacitors and resistors from datasheets; used a 21.6 V intermediate rail and designed the PCB layout in Altium.
Result
Produced the schematic, PCB layout and 3D board model. Physical board validation is a future step.
